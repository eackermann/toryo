## The app thread reservation, as it now stands

The reservation is `room.appTurn`, which `#appThreadWorking` checks across rooms (Slice 2's scheduler respects it). It starts when the app accepts the message (`onSubmitted`), or at recovery for a submitted or uncertain turn, and it's on disk. It ends only when the turn finishes, or when the owner lets it go (`app-release`, Stop twice). While a turn is being prepared or sent, the running turn itself reserves the thread. When following fails or times out, the turn becomes `lost` and stays kept, and the room asks the owner ("Follow it again"). A failure before anything was submitted keeps nothing: the crash path keeps an app turn only if it was submitted.

`test/boundary.test.mjs` (new, 9 tests; in-process agents, temporary homes):
- **Hold and pause during preparation** (both): snapshots are slowed, so a turn stays `preparing`. A hold (then Pause everything) applied then sends nothing: the turn is `deferred` with its reason and goes back to the queue, and the room is `held`. A second note while held is kept. Releasing sends **once**, with both notes in the prompt.
- **Already stopped before launch.** `runAgentProcess` with an aborted signal doesn't run its command (the file it would write never appears). `codexapp.run` with an aborted signal never runs the queue command, and `onSubmitting` isn't called.
- **No opening while held**: with a real `codexapp.run` (a fake `codex.exe`, a temporary log), `canOpen` false keeps the thread unopened for 500 ms; once it's true the thread opens and the turn completes.
- **One thread versus different threads**: rooms on t1, t2 and t1. t1 and t2 run together, and the second t1 room waits until the first t1 turn ends.
- **Following under a hold and a room pause, finishing during a restart.** The app has the message (submitted), then the app is held and the room paused, and Toryo stops. On restart, only a *follow* happens, with no send; the turn finishes, the thread frees, and the hold remains.
- **Repeated callbacks**: `onSubmitted` and `onStarted` twice each. The kept turn, `submittedAt` and `pickedUpAt` don't change, nothing is marked read twice, and the phase is `working`.
- **A crash mid-hand-over**: `onSubmitting` is called, then the desk stops before `onSubmitted`. After a restart, A's turn is followed, never sent; it's marked `uncertain` and the room has the warning. Room B's request on the same thread waits. When the app's turn appears and ends, B goes, and A's message was never sent a second time.
- **Following times out**: the app had the message and the follow reports a timeout. The turn is kept (`lost`) and the room asks; B waits. "Follow it again" follows, the turn ends, and B is sent.
- **Preparation fails** (the snapshot throws): A's turn fails with an error question and nothing is kept for it. B, waiting for the thread, goes at once.

## Worth a look

- **Only desk-wide restrictions defer a turn being prepared, not a room's Pause.** The plan's contract is "No unsent request passes a newly applied restriction" (desk and usage). A room's own Pause keeps its meaning: "the current turn finishes". A turn being prepared counts as current.
- **The uncertain hand-over isn't a question to the owner.** The room keeps working on what it can (nothing else goes to that thread), and the owner gets a warning and a notification, with Stop twice to let the turn go. A question would also hold up the room's other agents for something passive watching resolves by itself once the app sends.
- **Display of the phases** (*Preparing*, *Waiting in the Codex app*, *Working in the Codex app*) is Home's job (Slice 7). The data is there now: `live[].phase`, `submittedAt` and `pickedUpAt`.
- **The browser check** passes now. During the earlier rounds it stopped on another room's Settings error (Codex's L3: `fillModels` updated a sheet that might have closed while Cursor's model list loaded). That room has fixed it.
